Eurovision 2026

Semi-final survival notes from someone who watched both so you don’t have to.

Once again it is that time of year! Eurovision 2026 has arrived!

Brought to you by last year's winners Austria, the theme of this year's contest is "don't ask too many questions", like "Why do they keep referring to the Big 4?", "Has Rylan always co-hosted with Angela Scanlon?", and "Will a 25 second Boy George Cameo be enough to qualify for the finals?"

I have had the pleasure of watching both semi-finals to give you a quick overview of what to expect on the big day. What did you miss? Well, 10 countries didn't quite make the cut; there was an interval number about Austria sounding vaguely like Australia (featuring last years non-qualifier GoJo - the Milkshake Man); and we got a preview of new music from the previous winner JJ - an item normally reserved for the Grand Finals, so one can only assume JJ's new music has been trumped by an inevitable Conchita appearance at half time.

For those playing the Eurovision Drinking Game, some custom rules for this year:

  • Anything red and black
  • Silver outfits
  • Leather trousers

Cast in Order of Appearance

01. 🇩🇰 Denmark — Søren Torpegaard Lund – Før Vi Går Hjem

Look Denmark, if your main set piece is a big glass box, you're going to need to clean the box before you go on stage.

No one wants a grubby box.

02. 🇩🇪 Germany — Sarah Engels – Fire

A high-energy pop number featuring less actual German than the UK entry.

03. 🇮🇱 Israel — Noam Bettan – Michelle

A solid, catchy entry. Brave to include so many quiet sections.

04. 🇧🇪 Belgium — ESSYLA – Dancing on the Ice

My personal favourite this year, which historically does not bode well for its actual winning chances.

05. 🇦🇱 Albania — Alis – Nân

A special one. The refrain "Know Mother waits for you" was probably intended to be reassuring, however given the staging (complete with looming-floating-head of "Mother") you can't help but interpret it as a sinister threat. What have we done? and what will Mother do to us when our time comes? We can only pray for forgiveness.

06. 🇬🇷 Greece — Akylas – Ferto

An exceedingly repetitive assault on the senses, though it at least avoids the Cardinal Sin of Eurovision 1 .

07. 🇺🇦 Ukraine — LELÉKA – Ridnym

Hits the bingo cards of Traditional instrument, and an impossibly high spirit note in the crescendo. I also have a note here that reads 'strange finger gloves'.

08. 🇦🇺 Australia — Delta Goodrem – Eclipse

Look! It's Delta Goodrem!

09. 🇷🇸 Serbia — LAVINA – Kraj Mene

Eurovision is not complete without at least one screamy metal song. Officially has the seal of approval from Mr Lordi.

10. 🇲🇹 Malta — AIDAN – Bella

Malta's windswept crescendo was slightly undercut by the stage-tech with a leaf blower scurrying directly across camera the moment the song ended.

11. 🇨🇿 Czechia — Daniel Zizka – CROSSROADS

Just as I was preparing to accuse this song of committing the Cardinal Sin of Eurovision 1 , Rachael pointed out that Daniel Zizka appeared to be wearing two copies of the same shirt.

Whether this was a fashion statement, or backstage forgetfullness, we shall undoubtedly learn on Saturday.

12. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria — DARA – Bangaranga

A catchy tune with strong chairography. I expect this one will do rather well.

13. 🇭🇷 Croatia — LELEK – Andromeda

A wonderful entry landing on the surprisingly common folk-witches-curse-you-and-your-family end of the Eurovision spectrum

14. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom — LOOK MUM NO COMPUTER – Eins, Zwei, Drei

It took me a few listens for me to fully appreciate this Song; definitely among my favourites from this year's crop.

15. 🇫🇷 France — Monroe – Regarde !

With Regarde!, France is trying to replicate the success of Barbara Pavi's Voila! from 2021. One can only expect this trend of to continue, and as they iterate through the school vocabulary lists I look forward to 2031's Bibliotheque! and 2036's Baguette!

16. 🇲🇩 Moldova — Satoshi – Viva, Moldova!

A very catchy and upbeat entry, Moldova attempting to win this year with super-liminal messaging, which earns at least some begrudging respect.

17. 🇫🇮 Finland — Linda Lampenius x Pete Parkkonen – Liekinheitin

The favourites this year, and its easy to see why. An actual live violin performance too, with special permission from the EBU.

18. 🇵🇱 Poland — ALICJA – Pray

Some very impressive slope dancing. You'll see what I mean.

19. 🇱🇹 Lithuania — Lion Ceccah – Sólo Quiero Más

Would I like this song as much if the artist wasn't entirely painted from head to toe in Silver like one of those street performers? We'll never know.

20. 🇸🇪 Sweden — FELICIA – My System

A typically enjoyable entry from Sweden, although the semi-final performance was a little weak. Let's see if they can up their game for the final.

21. 🇨🇾 Cyprus — Antigoni – JALLA

Energetic and catchy, but in the same company as Sweden for a weak on-the-day performance in the semis.

22. 🇮🇹 Italy — Sal Da Vinci – Per Sempre Sì

Italy decided they did not want to win this year.

23. 🇳🇴 Norway — JONAS LOVV – YA YA YA

Ding ding dong?

24. 🇷🇴 Romania — Alexandra Căpitănescu – Choke Me

Would you be surprised if I told you this wasn't the only entry this year to feature guitar-tubes?

Because you should be surprised that this isn't the only entry this year to feature guitar-tubes.

25. 🇦🇹 Austria — COSMÓ – Tanzschein

Our hosts close the show on Saturday, with an entry that does seem to improve with every listen.
